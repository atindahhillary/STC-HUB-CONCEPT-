# SAVIT Tech Community Hub, Mombasa

Investor concept website for the SAVIT Tech Community Hub: five repurposed shipping containers in Utange, Kisauni, Mombasa, becoming a technology, climate and enterprise hub. Target opening May 2027. Proponent: SAVIT Global Solutions Limited.

Live site: https://atindahhillary.github.io/STC-HUB-CONCEPT-/

## What is here

- `index.html`: the pitch. Problem and opportunity, the eight-stage journey, an interactive isometric model of the five-container L (select a container to look inside), the open commons, SAVIT Global Academy programmes and a sample week, the innovation pipeline, the Waste Bridge circular-economy loop, the market and policy case, the business model with a live sustainability calculator, the investment ask and partner tiers, the roadmap to opening, impact targets, a six-lens concept review with a risk register, and the scale-up vision.
- `research.html`: the research annex, with the evidence and sources behind every figure.
- `assets/js/data.js`: all zone content, financial figures, targets and timeline data in one place. Update numbers here and the diagrams redraw.
- `assets/js/diagrams.js`: inline SVG renderers (isometric building, loop, funnel, Gantt, charts, Africa map).
- `assets/img/`: AI concept visualisations made in Canva, plus one Creative Commons photo of Mombasa Old Town (credited on the page).
- `assets/video/`: the hero background, a 14.5 second muted loop cut from "Aerial view of sand beach, top view sea waves" (Nature video, HD - 4K, CC BY 3.0, Wikimedia Commons), trimmed, slowed and looped. It pauses for reduced-motion and data-saver users and when scrolled out of view.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step. Ocean theme: sea-mist surfaces, deep-ocean ink, coral actions. Fonts: Inter (Google Fonts). Icons: Phosphor.

## About the figures

Budgets, revenues and targets are indicative desk estimates prepared in September 2026 for discussion, at KES 129.48 to the US dollar. Phase 1 replaces them with a quantity surveyor's bill of quantities, fabricator quotes and a signed site lease. Building and interior images are AI concept visualisations for illustration only.
